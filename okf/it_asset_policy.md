Purpose
This policy explains employee responsibilities for company-owned laptops, monitors, mobile devices,
software, and other IT assets.
Company Ownership
Laptops, monitors, mobile devices, and other IT equipment issued to employees remain company property.
Software
Employees should install only approved software on company devices. Unauthorized software may introduce
security or licensing risks.
Care of Assets
Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of assigned equipment and should avoid leaving
company devices unattended in unsafe locations.
Passwords and Access
Employees must protect device passwords and should not allow another person to use their company
account.
Damage or Loss
Lost, stolen, or damaged equipment should be reported to the IT support team as soon as possible.
Repairs
Employees should not attempt unauthorized hardware repairs. Equipment problems should be reported
through the approved IT support process.
Return
Company IT assets must be returned when an employee leaves the company, changes role when required,
or when IT requests the equipment.
Data Removal
Employees should not independently delete or transfer company data before returning an IT asset. IT will
follow the applicable asset-return process.
