Purpose
This policy defines basic security requirements for employees who access company systems or information
outside approved office locations.
Company Device
Employees working remotely should use company-approved devices for company work. Personal devices
should not be used for restricted company information unless explicitly authorized.
Network Security
Employees should use the approved secure network or VPN when accessing internal company systems
remotely.
Passwords
Employees must protect passwords and authentication credentials and should never share them with another
person.
Data Protection
Company and customer data must not be stored on personal devices or shared through unauthorized cloud
services or messaging applications.
Screen Security
Employees should lock their screen when leaving the device unattended and should take reasonable steps to
prevent unauthorized people from viewing confidential information.
Public Locations
Employees should avoid discussing confidential information or displaying sensitive documents in public
locations where other people can observe them.
Security Incidents
Lost devices, suspected data exposure, phishing attempts, or other security incidents should be reported to
the security team immediately.
Software Updates
Company devices should receive required security updates and approved endpoint protection should remain
enabled.
