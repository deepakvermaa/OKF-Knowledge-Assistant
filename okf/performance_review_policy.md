Purpose
This policy describes the company's general approach to performance reviews, goal setting, feedback, and
performance ratings.
Review Cycle
Employees normally receive a formal performance review once each year. Some teams may conduct
additional reviews or checkpoints during the year.
Goal Setting
Performance goals should be agreed between the employee and reporting manager at the beginning of the
review period. Goals should be clear enough to measure progress.
Regular Feedback
Managers are encouraged to provide feedback throughout the year instead of waiting for the formal review.
Self Assessment
Employees may be asked to provide a self-assessment describing achievements, challenges, learning, and
progress against agreed goals.
Performance Rating
Performance ratings consider achievement of goals, quality of work, collaboration, reliability, and professional
behavior. The exact rating scale may be defined by the organization.
Improvement Plan
When performance does not meet expectations, the manager may define an improvement plan with clear
expectations, support, and review dates.
Promotion
Strong performance does not automatically guarantee promotion. Promotion decisions also depend on role
availability, business requirements, skills, and organizational processes.
