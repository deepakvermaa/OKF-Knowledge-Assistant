# Employee Leave Policy

1. Employees are entitled to 15 days of annual leave per calendar year.
2. Up to 3 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the following year.
3. Planned leave should normally be requested at least 3 working days in advance.
4. For leave longer than 5 working days, employees should discuss the request with their manager first.
5. Sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible.
6. Employees should update leave status in the approved HR system.
7. Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave.
8. Managers review requests based on team workload.
9. This version was effective from January 1, 2025.
10. Version 1.0 has been superseded by version 2.0.
