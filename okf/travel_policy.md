Purpose
This policy defines the basic rules for employee travel undertaken for approved business purposes.
Travel Approval
Business travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager before transportation or
accommodation is booked.
Booking
Employees should use the approved company travel process whenever it is available. Travel should be
booked early enough to obtain reasonable fares.
Flights
Economy class is the standard class for domestic business travel. Any exception requires appropriate
approval before booking.
Rail Travel
Train travel may be used when it is practical for the business trip. Employees should select a reasonable
class based on company travel guidelines.
Accommodation
Hotel accommodation should normally be booked through the approved travel process. The selected hotel
should be reasonably priced and suitable for the business purpose.
Local Transport
Reasonable taxi, public transport, or other approved local transportation costs may be claimed when they are
necessary for the business trip.
Changes and Cancellation
Employees should cancel or change bookings as soon as travel plans change. Avoidable cancellation costs
may require manager review.
Personal Travel
Personal travel added to a business trip must not create an unreasonable additional cost for the company.
Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
