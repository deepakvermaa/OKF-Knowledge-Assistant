Purpose
This policy explains the general process for claiming reasonable expenses incurred for approved business
activities.
Eligible Expenses
Reasonable expenses directly related to approved business activities may be reimbursed when they follow
company limits and approval requirements.
Receipts
Employees should keep receipts, invoices, and other supporting documents for expense claims.
Approval
Expense claims require approval according to the applicable department approval process. Employees
should not approve their own expenses when separation of duties is required.
Submission
Claims should be submitted through the approved expense system within the applicable submission period.
Business Meals
Business meals may be reimbursed when they are for a valid business purpose and comply with applicable
limits. The business purpose should be recorded where required.
Personal Expenses
Personal shopping, personal subscriptions, fines, penalties, and expenses unrelated to approved business
activity are not reimbursable.
Missing Receipts
When a receipt cannot be obtained, the employee should follow the company's documented exception
process. Missing documentation may result in rejection of the claim.
Review
Finance may request additional information or return a claim for correction before reimbursement.
