Purpose
The code of conduct defines expected professional behavior and provides general principles for maintaining a
respectful and responsible workplace.
Professional Behavior
Employees are expected to behave respectfully and professionally toward colleagues, customers, vendors,
and business partners.
Confidentiality
Employees must protect confidential company and customer information and should access information only
when required for their work.
Conflicts of Interest
Employees should disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest through the appropriate company process.
Gifts and Hospitality
Employees should follow company rules regarding gifts, hospitality, and interactions with customers or
vendors. Items that could influence a business decision should not be accepted.
Harassment
Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and threatening behavior are not acceptable in the workplace.
Company Resources
Company systems, equipment, and information should be used responsibly and primarily for authorized
business purposes.
Reporting Concerns
Employees who become aware of serious misconduct should use the appropriate reporting or escalation
channel.
Compliance
Employees are expected to follow applicable laws, company policies, and professional standards relevant to
their work.
