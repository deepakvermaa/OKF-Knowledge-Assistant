Purpose
This policy explains the types of leave available to employees, the normal approval process, and the
responsibilities of employees and managers.
Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of annual leave during each calendar year. Annual leave is intended for planned
personal time, vacation, and other non-medical purposes. Employees should normally submit a request at
least 3 working days before the planned start date.
Sick Leave
Employees may take up to 10 days of sick leave in a calendar year. Sick leave should be used when an
employee is unable to work because of illness or a medical appointment. A medical certificate may be
requested when an absence continues for more than 2 consecutive working days.
Casual Leave
Employees may take up to 6 days of casual leave during the year for short personal requirements. Casual
leave should normally be requested before the absence and must be approved by the reporting manager.
Public Holidays
Public holidays are handled according to the company's published holiday calendar. A public holiday does not
normally reduce the employee's annual leave balance.
Leave Approval
Employees must submit leave requests through the employee portal. The reporting manager reviews the
request based on business requirements and available leave balance. Approval should be obtained before
planned leave begins.
Emergency Leave
For an unexpected illness or emergency, the employee should inform the manager as soon as reasonably
possible. The formal leave request should be completed after the immediate situation is handled.
Cancellation
If approved leave is no longer required, the employee should cancel the request in the employee portal so
that the leave balance can be corrected.
