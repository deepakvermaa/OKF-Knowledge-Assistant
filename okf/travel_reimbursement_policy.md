Purpose
This policy explains which reasonable business-travel expenses can be reimbursed and what supporting
information is required.
Hotel Expenses
Employees may claim reasonable hotel expenses incurred during approved business travel. A valid hotel
invoice or equivalent supporting document should be provided.
Meals
Reasonable meal expenses during approved business travel may be reimbursed according to applicable
company limits. Personal entertainment expenses are not included.
Local Transport
Taxi, public transport, parking, and other approved local travel costs may be claimed when they are directly
related to the business trip.
Air and Rail Tickets
Approved transportation costs may be reimbursed when the booking follows company travel requirements
and the expense is supported by the required document.
Receipts
Employees should retain receipts and invoices for expenses. If a receipt is unavailable, the employee should
follow the company's exception process rather than creating unsupported documentation.
Claim Deadline
Travel reimbursement claims should normally be submitted within 15 days after the employee returns from
the trip.
Approval
Expense claims are reviewed by the employee's manager and the applicable finance process. Claims may be
returned when information or supporting documents are missing.
Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal shopping, minibar purchases, leisure activities, fines, and expenses unrelated to the approved
business purpose are not reimbursable.
