# Travel Reimbursement Policy

1. Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim eligible travel expenses.
2. Business travel should be approved by the manager before the trip whenever possible.
3. Hotel reimbursement is limited to INR 4,000 per night for standard business travel.
4. Local transportation expenses may be claimed with valid receipts.
5. Employees should retain original or approved digital receipts.
6. Expense claims should normally be submitted within 15 days after the trip.
7. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
8. Expenses above the normal limit require the required approval.
9. All claims are subject to manager approval and finance rules.
10. Incomplete claims may be returned for correction.
